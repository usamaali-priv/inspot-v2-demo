Jewellery questions

Will the colour fade?

Plated jewellery keeps its colour longest when it stays dry and away from perfume and lotion. With that care, plating usually lasts 6–12 months of regular wear.

Can I wear it in water?

Take it off before washing, swimming or the gym. Water, sweat and soap dull plating.

What if the size is wrong?

Exchange it within 7 days. WhatsApp us your order number and a photo, and we reply within 24 hours.
